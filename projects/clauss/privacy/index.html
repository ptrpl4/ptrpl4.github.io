Privacy Policy

Clauss · Last updated 9 August 2026

Clauss does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app accesses

Clauss runs entirely on your Mac. It reads Claude Code's session files stored locally on the device in order to list and manage them. That information is only ever shown on the device and is never sent anywhere. App settings are saved locally using macOS user defaults.

Data you provide

Clauss has no account, sign-in, or server. It does not ask for or receive any personal information.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
