Privacy Policy

FIGa · Last updated 9 August 2026

FIGa does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app accesses

FIGa filters network content entirely on your device using a local DNS filter. DNS queries are evaluated on-device against local blocklists; they are not logged, transmitted, or shared, and no browsing history leaves the device. App settings and blocklists are stored locally.

Data you provide

FIGa has no account, sign-in, or server. It does not ask for or receive any personal information.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
